### Hi 👋, I'm A H M Osama Haque

I'm a full-time Lecturer at United International University. I completed my graduation in Compter Science and Engineering (CSE) from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). My work bridges computer science and clinical relevance, aiming to build <b>trustworthy AI systems</b> for healthcare decision support. I have developed hybrid architectures that fuse 3D medical images (CT, MRI) with clinical and molecular data for disease prediction and survival analysis, particularly in <b>lung cancer and Alzheimer’s disease</b>. I specialize in analyzing feature interactions, temporal dynamics of biomarkers, and explainable AI techniques to uncover meaningful patterns that clinicians can interpret. Beyond medical imaging, I also explore morphometric and geometric representations, survival models, Vision Transformers, and transformer-based architectures for complex biological data. My projects span simulation, biological feature engineering, and IoT-based intelligent systems, demonstrating my versatility and ability to build end-to-end AI solutions. Currently, I am a Lecturer in the Department of CSE at United International University, and I completed my B.Sc. in CSE from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) with a CGPA of 3.90/4.00. I am passionate about advancing AI-driven discovery in healthcare through interpretable, multimodal, and biologically grounded models, and I aim to pursue a Ph.D. in this direction.
